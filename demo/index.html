@issuegraph/viewer and @issuegraph/editor on a dense in-memory backlog. No tracker, no credentials, no backend. Every edit is client-side and saved nowhere; reload to get the seed back.

An armed outcome fires once and disarms itself, so the retry it offers can actually land.

The order is @issuegraph/derive’s, not this page’s: the store takes the deriver as a port with no default, and this page fills it with the published derivation rather than a second reading of the ordering rules. The rail, the canvas and the inspector are the editor’s; the page reads the commands they publish, reduces, and renders again. What is still the host’s is what the specification puts there — the executor holds (§6.8), the concurrency cap (§6.5), the words, and the theme. Both themes are the viewer’s own custom properties redeclared, and nothing else.
